What it is

Lumi Letters Pro is a phonics and alphabet app for young learners: hear the sound, find it in the word, trace the letter. Children travel through 14 planets with 12 question types and collect 26 letter friends.

  • Letter name and letter sound first, then beginning, ending and middle sounds
  • Uppercase and lowercase tracing with stroke guides
  • 14 planets, 12 question types, daily challenge and time race
  • Parent Zone with four skills (letter recognition, alphabet order, phonemic awareness, picture phonics)
  • Printable PDF worksheet with the letters practiced that day

Who it is for

  • Parents of children who are learning English letters and sounds
  • Families who want no ads, no subscription and no data collection
  • Parents who want to see which skills are strong and which need more practice

How it is paid for

Lumi Letters Pro is a paid download with a one-time purchase. There is no subscription, no recurring charge and no nagging in-app purchases: you buy once and get everything. Check the current price on the store page.

Frequently asked questions

Is Lumi Letters Pro a subscription?

No. It is a one-time purchase with no subscription and no recurring charge.

What is phonics here?

Children hear a word and find its beginning, middle or end sound, then trace the letter.

How many planets and question types are there?

14 planets and 12 question types, plus 26 letter friends to collect.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Progress is stored on the device and the app works without internet.

Can I print a worksheet?

Yes. The Parent Zone exports a PDF worksheet with the letters practiced that day.

Are there ads?

No ads and no data collection.
